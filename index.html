Research & academic profile

Precision nutrition.
Computational biology.
Human health.

My research focuses on precision nutrition and the relationships between diet, the gut microbiome, and human health. As a Ph.D. candidate in Nutritional Biology at UC Davis, I combine clinical nutrition research with bioinformatics and AI-assisted dietary assessment to investigate how dietary exposures relate to microbial composition, function, and health outcomes. My current work includes integrating food composition databases, developing tools to digitize dietary records, and evaluating sequencing strategies for metagenomic and metatranscriptomic studies. Through these projects, I aim to improve how we characterize diet–microbiome interactions and inform nutritional approaches to healthy aging, with a particular interest in retinal health.
